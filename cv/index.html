PhD Researcher · IPLab, University of Catania, Catania, Italy Aug 2023 – Jul 2026
SAMOTHRACE project · AI for wearables and cognitive bias in forensics
  • Built ORION, an end-to-end pipeline that detects smoking episodes from wrist motion sensors by fusing 7 classifiers; on 40 participants (leave-one-subject-out) it reached an event-level F1 of 0.966 with no false positives in 29.5 hours of recordings (under review)
  • Introduced the impostor bias, a systematic doubt that real media are authentic, and validated it with 746 participants whose accuracy at spotting AI images sat at chance (47–55.5%)
  • Co-built two forensic benchmarks, WILD (20,000 images, 20 generators) and SynthForensics (6,815 videos, 5 text-to-video models; NeurIPS 2026), on which state-of-the-art detectors lose 29 AUC points on average
  • Co-developed an on-device autism screening pipeline from children's gaze, distilled into a 1.6M-parameter ONNX ensemble that runs offline in an open-source Android app
  • Led scoping reviews on AI chatbots for mental health (300+ citations, cited in UK Parliament POSTnote 737), wearables for smoking detection (49 studies, IEEE JBHI) and AI for eating disorders (7,545 records ranked by an LLM)
