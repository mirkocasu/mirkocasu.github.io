Mirko Casu

AI Researcher and Licensed Psychologist.
I study the human–AI relationship and its clinical and forensic applications.

Profile

PhD in Computer Science at the University of Catania (IPLab, 2023–2026), recently a visiting researcher at EPFL, and a licensed clinical psychologist. I introduced the concept of impostor bias, a systematic suspicion that what we see online is AI-generated, fuelled by our awareness of what generative AI can do. My work now centres on language models and AI agents, with a focus on mental health and AI safety.
